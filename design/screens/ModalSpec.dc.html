Modal header and footer

The top and bottom of every modal. The box itself (scrim, bg.elevated, radius 16, modal shadow, placed from the top) and its body stay in the screen. ModalHeader: serif 22px title, a 13px muted sub (subMono 11px for a repo / branch or a path), padding 20px 24px 14px, optional ✕. ModalFooter: bg.sunken, padding 14px 24px, a 12px muted note or a lead link button on the left, an optional aside, then buttons (Button props, 36px), the primary last; ruled false when the list above ends in a row border.
