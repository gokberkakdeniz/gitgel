Menu

Popover menus. bg.elevated, radius 12, padding 6, popover shadow; items 8px 10px, radius 8, the hovered one bg.hover. An item may lead with a line icon, an app icon, a radio check, a checkbox or a dot; carry a mono sub or a 12px desc line; end with a hint, a mono kbd or an arrow; be disabled, danger or accent. Between items: separators, section labels, warning notes, captions and a segmented row. Each row is a MenuItem (its own artboard, below). The screen positions the menu.
